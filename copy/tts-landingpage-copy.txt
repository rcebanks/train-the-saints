# **Open Letter Section**

## A. Welcome Section

Heading Text: “Welcome!” 

Body Text: “My name is Rachelle Ebanks and I am a former high school teacher and ministry leader who is stepping out of her comfort zone to follow the spirit of God in this new endeavor called Train the Saints.” 

## B. Body Section

Heading Text: “For the advancement of Christ’s kingdom!”

Body Text: “I have been noticing a pattern that has created a holy disturbance in me: Why are there so many Christians who have been serving God for 10, 20, 30 years, yet are still spiritually immature? 

Is it the lack of teaching? Is it too much comfort? Is it the lack of close relationships? Is it because we are too integrated with the culture? What is it? I must know. We must know. It is imperative. God’s holy and beautiful kingdom must be brought down into this dark and suffering world and we need mature Christians to make it happen. 

1 Corinthians 12 says that the church is a body and there are many parts and right now, my part is to empower leaders with a clear and comprehensive understanding of how people spiritually mature. I created a survey on spiritual maturity that should take approximately 5-10 minutes of your time. My hope is that we can reach thousands of American Christians so that we can build a playbook for spiritual maturity and advance the kingdom of Jesus Christ.”
